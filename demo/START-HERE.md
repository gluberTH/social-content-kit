# Start here

Open index.html locally. Everything works offline except external destination links. No account connection or web server is needed.

For each post, upload its image, paste caption.txt, and enter alt-text.txt in the accessibility field. For Pins, use title.txt, description.txt, and destination-link.txt separately. Read publish-notes.txt for network-specific steps.

The calendar includes local and UTC dates. Use the specified timezone in your own scheduler if you later choose to schedule. No posts have been sent or scheduled by this package.

Review QA_REPORT.md and current product conditions before publishing. Keep measured results in results-tracker.csv.
